This page holds the material that did not fit on the poster: the full stability derivation behind the numerical scheme, the convergence and cost study, and the complete controller-robustness results including the case where the method does not work well.

The work solves a partial integro-differential equation (PIDE) for the protein distribution of a self-regulating gene, with kinetic parameters treated as random variables. An intrusive polynomial chaos expansion (PCE) and a Galerkin projection turn the stochastic PIDE into a coupled deterministic system for the PCE coefficients, discretised with summation-by-parts (SBP) operators and simultaneous-approximation-term (SAT) boundary conditions.
